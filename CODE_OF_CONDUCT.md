# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Nevermoire respectful and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience,
or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, copyright, and consent when sharing photographs.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualised attention,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainers through
the contact method on their GitHub profiles. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participation
when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
